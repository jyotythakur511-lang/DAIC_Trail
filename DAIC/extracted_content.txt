FIRST STEP TO GET MORE BUSINESS
Step 1: Define your services
Create a clear list of services, for example:
Snowflake data migration 
SQL Server to Snowflake 
Oracle to Snowflake 
ETL development 
Data pipeline development 
Data warehouse migration 
Data validation and reconciliation 
Performance optimization 
Step 2: Build a portfolio
Even if you can't share client work:
Create 3-5 demo projects. 
Show the architecture. 
Explain the migration process. 
Mention the tools used (Snowflake, SQL, Python, Airflow, Azure, AWS, etc.). 
Upload them to GitHub or create PDFs. 
Step 3: Create company profiles
Register on:
Upwork 
Freelancer 
Toptal (if eligible) 
Fiverr 
LinkedIn Services 
Guru 
People Per Hour 
Use your company name and highlight your team's experience.
Step 4: Prepare proposal templates
Write a professional proposal that includes:
Understanding of the client's requirements. 
Your approach. 
Timeline. 
Estimated cost. 
Previous relevant experience. 
Support after delivery. 
Step 5: Start with smaller projects
Initially bid on projects worth around $100–500. Your goal is to build positive reviews. Once you have several 5-star reviews, you can target larger projects.
Step 6: Optimize LinkedIn
Post regularly about:
Snowflake 
Data migration 
ETL 
SQL tips 
Case studies 
Project success stories 
This helps attract potential clients organically.
Step 7: Build a simple website
Include:
Services 
Team expertise 
Technologies 
Contact form 
Case studies 
Client testimonials (when available) 
Step 8: Create a delivery process
Standardize your workflow:
Requirement gathering 
Proposal 
Planning 
Development 
Testing 
Data validation 
Deployment 
Documentation 
Post-production support 
Step 9: Price your services
You can offer:
Fixed-price projects 
Hourly rates 
Monthly support retainers 
Step 10: Ask for referrals
After completing each project:
Request a review. 
Ask for referrals. 
Offer ongoing maintenance contracts. 
Extra suggestion
Since your expertise is in data migration, don't rely only on freelance platforms. Many companies hire directly through LinkedIn. Connect with:
CTOs 
Data Engineering Managers 
Data Architects 
Startup founders 
IT consultants
